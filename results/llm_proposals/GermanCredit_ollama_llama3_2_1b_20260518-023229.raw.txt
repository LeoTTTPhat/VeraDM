# LLM Hypothesis Proposal Prompt

### Hypotheses

```json
{

    "IF x0 = 1 THEN y = 1 increases",
    "IF x1 = 1 AND x4 = 1 THEN y = 1 decreases",
    "IF x2 > 24 THEN y = 1 increases",
    "IF x3 = 1 AND x5 = 1 THEN y = 1 decreases",

```

These hypotheses are generated based on the provided feature IDs and discovery-set summaries, ensuring that they adhere to the verifier's grammar and rules.
